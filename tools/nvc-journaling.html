NVC Journaling

A self-empathy walk through what I feel and need, and what I imagine you feel and need.

This is a self-guided journaling tool — there is no AI assistant, no sign-in, and nothing you write here is sent anywhere or saved on any server. Everything stays on this page until you close or print it; that privacy is literally true, not a promise layered on top of something that phones home. Ported from an earlier self-guided NVC journaling tool, itself inspired by NYC NVC's self-empathy process. The feelings and needs lists below are fetched live from this library's own nvc-needs school, not hardcoded on this page — add a feeling there and it appears here.
