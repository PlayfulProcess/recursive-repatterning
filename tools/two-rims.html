Two Rims

A working model of a couple, one dial at a time. Nine steps. Each one asks you to guess before it shows you — because a guess you have committed to is the only way to find out what you actually believed.

By the end you will have moved every dial yourself, and you will know why a model can explain a marriage beautifully and still not predict one.

What this is and isn't. This is psychoeducational, not clinical. The model has never met anyone. It cannot tell you anything about your relationship, it does not measure or score you, and nothing you type here leaves your browser. The two people in it are called A and B — not husband and wife, and not you.

    One more thing worth carrying out of here. The pattern that survives across the competing models — not any of their origin stories — is demand/withdraw: one partner pressing while the other pulls back, at around r = .36 with dissatisfaction. The schools disagree about why couples hurt. They agree remarkably well about what it looks like.
