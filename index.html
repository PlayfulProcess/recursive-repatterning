The short version

A working interface, not a discovery

There are no fixed biological fingerprints for emotions, and we still have to make meaning — so every school here is a working interface. Plutchik's wheel, NVC's needs, DBT's skills, attachment theory's bonds: the constructionist account sits underneath all of them, and that is precisely why the interfaces matter. A map being constructed is not the same as a map being wrong.

Each school is presented faithfully, in its own terms — an NVC section sounds like NVC, not like us editorializing about it. And the hard floor everywhere: a feeling-name is a mirror, never a diagnosis, and never something to obey. Read the full creed →

One branch of a larger tree

recursive.eco is the trunk this library grows from — an open platform built on grammars: symbolic systems you make meaning with — made by people, not algorithms (a school of emotion, an I Ching, an astrology set, a children's story). A school of emotion is a grammar, and this whole site is one of them: a branch able to render the deep, domain-specific richness — the evidence, the citations, the per-item research — that the general platform never could.

The trunk gives every branch more than it could grow alone: an AI Oracle that reads your reading in context, a journal, accounts, and the power to build your own school and use it as a living library.
